Frequently asked questions

A few useful details before your first session.

What do I need to start learning?

Create an account, select a supported AI provider, and add your own provider API key in Settings. LearnOrbit sends tutor requests through its backend to that provider.

Can I learn any subject?

Start a session with a topic of your choice. The tutor can explain concepts, work through examples, and answer follow-up questions. Always verify high-stakes information with a trusted source.

How does LearnOrbit measure progress?

Session history, quiz results, and topic mastery are based on activity saved to your account. Academy adds confidence and recall summaries with the calculation evidence shown alongside them.

Are subscriptions and payments live?

No. The billing screen is a demonstration checkout and does not collect or process real payments. A plan change only controls features within this app.

Are my notes and conversations public?

Your learning sessions and notes are tied to your account. Do not enter sensitive personal information into a chat; requests are also sent to the AI provider configured for your account.
